Argon Incorporated LLC is fully aligned with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and processes personal data in accordance with its principles of transparency, fairness, and accountability.
Our GDPR commitments include:
•	Lawful Basis for Processing: We process personal data only when we have a valid legal basis under Article 6 of the GDPR, including (a) your consent, (b) necessity for contract performance, (c) compliance with legal obligations, (d) legitimate interests pursued by Argon, or (e) protection of vital interests.
•	Data Minimization: We collect and process only the minimum personal data required to achieve the stated purposes.
•	Purpose Limitation: Data is used strictly for the reasons it was collected and not for unrelated activities.
•	User Rights: Under the GDPR, users located in the European Economic Area (EEA) have enhanced rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection to processing. These rights may be exercised through our support or privacy contact channels.
•	Data Protection Officer (DPO): Argon maintains an internal Data Protection Officer responsible for ensuring GDPR compliance and handling inquiries regarding data protection.
•	International Transfers: For transfers outside the EEA, Argon applies Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or other approved legal mechanisms to maintain adequate protection.
We continuously review and update our data protection practices to ensure ongoing compliance with GDPR and related data protection frameworks.
